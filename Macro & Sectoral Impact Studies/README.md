It contains 3 projects about macroeconomic and sectoral impact studies. And they are

    • COVID-19 Economic Resilience (TCS YEP): Prepared comprehensive overviews on sectoral disruption across Indian agriculture, manufacturing, and services sectors, delivering summary policy observations.
