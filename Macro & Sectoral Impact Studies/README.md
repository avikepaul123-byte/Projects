It contains 3 projects about macroeconomic and sectoral impact studies. And they are

    • COVID-19 Economic Resilience (TCS YEP, 2021): Prepared comprehensive overviews on sectoral disruption across Indian agriculture, manufacturing, and services sectors, delivering summary policy observations.
    • Finance-Growth Nexus (2020): Conducted an empirical comparative study across developed and emerging economies investigating bidirectional causality between financial development indicators and GDP growth.
    • Union Budget Infrastructure Review (2020): Reviewed capital expenditure allocations and infrastructure schemes in Union Budget 2020.
